Company Employee Policy

Employees receive 18 days of annual paid leave.

Employees can work remotely for up to 2 days per week.

The standard probation period is 6 months.

Employees must provide 30 days notice before resignation.

Working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Employees are eligible for health insurance after completing the probation period.

The company provides 12 casual leave days per year.

Employees must maintain confidentiality of company information.
